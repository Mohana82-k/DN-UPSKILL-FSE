Help & Support

Welcome to the Local Community Event Portal help page.

How to use the portal:

  • Registration: Fill in your name, email, select an event date and type, then submit.
  • Feedback: Enter your phone number (validated on blur), choose an event to see the fee, write your feedback, and submit.
  • Event Gallery: Click on any image to enlarge it.
  • Promo Video: Watch the community video – it will show "Video ready to play" once loaded.
  • Preferences: Your selected event type is saved automatically. Use "Clear Preferences" to remove saved data.
  • Nearby Events: Click the geolocation button to find events near you (requires permission).

If you encounter issues, please contact us at info@communityportal.com.
