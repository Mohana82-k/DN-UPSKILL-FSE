📰 Community Bulletin

The annual Summer Festival returns this July! Food trucks, live bands, and a kids' zone will fill the downtown area. Volunteers are needed – sign up online. The community garden project is looking for green thumbs. Contact the council to reserve a plot. The library will host a digital skills workshop every Tuesday in June. Free for all residents. The city council approved a new bike lane on Elm Street. Construction begins in August. Also, the recycling center will open on Saturdays from 9am to 1pm. Let's keep our neighborhood clean. A new dog park is planned for the west side. Donations are welcome. The local history museum has a new exhibit on the 1920s. Open daily except Mondays.
